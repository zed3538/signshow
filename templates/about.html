Inspiration

SignShow began as a final project for GirlBoss Edge: Tech for Good in 2024. The project was among the Top 30 Finalists of the programme. It remained as a simple wireframe until the creation of this website.

The project requirements was centred around inclusivity and accessibility of technology for people with disabilities. It was originally a sign language translation and learning application with the added feature of gesture-based translation from the camera. SignShow has yet to implement this, but there are hopes in the future to do so.

New Zealand Sign Language (NZSL) is one of New Zealand's official languages, and I believe it is important for us to acknowledge that.
